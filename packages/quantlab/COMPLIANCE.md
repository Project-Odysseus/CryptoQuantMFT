# Compliance and ownership note

**What this is.** quantlab is a research and trading-system library written by its author on the author's own time and
own equipment.

**What it contains.** Code, documentation and tests only. It contains no employer data, code, models, client
information or other confidential material, and must never receive any:
- no employer files, exports or screenshots copied in;
- no code written on an employer's time or systems;
- no parameters or ideas taken from non-public employer work.

**Data.** Market data is fetched from public, free sources by adapters, at run time, into caches that are not part
of the package. Data licensed by a future employer stays in that employer's systems. The lab reads it through an
adapter configured there, and that data is never committed here.

**Trading.**
- Under the author's current employment, personal trading is restricted to crypto assets. Equity, rates, FX and
  commodity research in this lab is research and paper trading only.
- Any live use for non-crypto markets requires the relevant employer's or fund's permission and compliance
  approval, at that firm, under its rules.
- Personal-trading rules of any future employer apply in full.

**Ownership.** All rights reserved by the author. The package is proprietary; it is not licensed for use by others.
Contributions from third parties must not be accepted without a written assignment.

**If employment changes.**
- Before using the lab at a new firm, review its intellectual-property and outside-activity terms.
- Keep a dated record (the git history) showing the code predates the new role.
- Run the lab there on that firm's infrastructure and data, through adapters, without copying firm data back.
