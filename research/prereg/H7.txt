PRE-REGISTRATION H7: TREND RULES LOSE AFTER A FLAT STRETCH, SO HALVE THEM THERE
Version 1. Written 2026-10-04, before any H7 test was run. Changes after results = a new version (lock it again), logged
as extra trials.

PRIOR EXPOSURE (what has already been looked at that bears on this)
- 2026-10-04, regime_study.py on the multi-strategy book, 2022-10-10 to 2025-12-31: days labelled "flat" (definition
  below) had the trend strategies at -24% to -78% a year (Sharpe -0.7 to -2.3) and the whole book at -36%; on
  "trending" days they made +59% to +147%. By year, the whole book on flat days: -190%, +14%, -43%, -17%. This is
  where the hypothesis comes from, so that window cannot test it.
- The label's windows (60, 30, 365 days) were fixed before that look and are unchanged here.
- Not looked at: the same split before 2022-10-10. That is the test sample.

MARKET BEHAVIOUR
After 60 days in which Bitcoin went nowhere through a lot of back and forth, trend and breakout rules keep losing:
the state persists, and each false start costs a round trip.

MECHANISM
A trend rule earns from long moves and pays for every reversal. A range that has held for two months tends to hold a
while longer, so the next signals are more likely false starts. The other side is whoever provides liquidity inside
the range.

OBSERVABLE PREDICTION AND EXPECTED SIGN
On days labelled flat, the trend book's return is negative and lower than on other days.

DATA
- Kraken Futures BTC/USD and ETH/USD perpetual daily candles (the runtime's own series), through
  src/portfolio/backtest.run_book with the config's costs and funding.
- Strategies: btc_ma_1d, btc_keltner_ls, eth_ma_1d, eth_keltner_ls exactly as in config/portfolio.multi_paper.toml,
  equal weight, no risk overlay. Their daily return is "the trend book".
- Label input: Binance BTCUSDT perpetual daily closes (src/research/pit.py), from 2019-09.
- TEST SAMPLE: the first day with a label to 2022-10-09. The later window is reported next to it, marked as the
  window the hypothesis came from. The frozen holdout (2026 on) stays locked.

LABEL (unchanged from regime_study.py)
Efficiency ratio = |close - close 60 days earlier| / sum of the 60 absolute daily changes. Its percentile rank among
its own previous 365 values (at least 182). "Flat" = rank <= 1/3. The label for a day uses closes up to two days
before it, so it is known before that day's return starts.

RULE
Gated book: the trend book at half size on flat days, full size otherwise. Every change of size costs 0.05% of
equity (half the book traded at 5 bps fee plus 5 bps slippage).

PRIMARY OUTCOME
The difference, in the test sample, between the trend book's mean daily return on flat days and on all other days,
annualised, with a 95% interval from a circular block bootstrap of the daily series (20-day blocks, 2,000 draws,
seed 0).

SECONDARY OUTCOMES
- The trend book's return and Sharpe on flat days, per strategy and per year.
- Gated against ungated over the test sample: Sharpe, return, worst drawdown; the number of size changes.
- The same numbers for 2022-10-10 to 2025-12-31 (seen before; for reference).

PARAMETER GRID
1 trial: the label and rule above. Nothing else is run.

PASS CRITERIA (all three, on the test sample)
1. The trend book's mean return on flat days is negative.
2. The flat-minus-other difference is negative and its 95% interval excludes 0.
3. The gated book's Sharpe is at least the ungated book's, after the switching cost.

KILL IF
The trend book's mean return on flat days is higher than on other days in the test sample.
Between kill and pass: inconclusive, and the gate is not built.

WHAT A PASS LEADS TO
A gate in the portfolio engine is a change to how the paper books trade. A pass makes it a candidate: it is then
added to a paper book only, as a config option that is off by default, and judged there. Not live.

KNOWN RISKS AND BIASES
- The test sample is about two years and is mostly one bull run and one bear market. Few flat stretches, so low power.
- The label is one definition chosen without tuning; another reasonable one might disagree.
- The four strategies are highly correlated: this is close to one series, not four.
- Halving on flat days also halves the gains when a range ends in a breakout, which is when trend rules earn most.
