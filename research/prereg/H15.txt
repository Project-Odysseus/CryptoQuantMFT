PRE-REGISTRATION H15: THE H13 TREND DESIGN EARNS A SIGNIFICANT RETURN ACROSS EQUITY, TREASURY, CRUDE OIL AND CURRENCY FUTURES
Version 2. Version 1 was written 2026-10-20, before any H15 test was run and before any price of the Treasury, crude oil
or Russell 2000 series was looked at in this project. Changes after results = a new version (lock it again), logged as
extra trials.

VERSION 2 (2026-10-20, after the version 1 run): the only change is the currencies' data source. Version 1 used Yahoo spot
rates, which have bad ticks (EUR/USD +17% on 2008-12-08 and JPY/USD +18% on 2008-12-09; true moves were about 2%), found
by a data check after the run. Version 2 uses Norges Bank fixings crossed to US dollars (X/NOK over USD/NOK, as H11).
Version 1's result, kept on record: strategy card "no evidence", Sharpe 0.35, t 1.61, halves 0.56 and 0.13: inconclusive.
Its trials stay counted.

PURPOSE
The owner asked (2026-10-20) to run the trend model on a mix of Treasury, equity index, energy and currency futures (the
CME contracts Kraken offers, priced from the free source), as a paper book next to the equity-trend book. Research and
paper only: the owner may not trade these markets personally (employer rule), so nothing here can lead to a live book.
The design is H13's primary variant P unchanged (research/prereg/H13.txt, R. J. Martin 2023): only the markets change.

PRIOR EXPOSURE
- H13 (2026-10-19): P on the Nasdaq-100, S&P 500 and STOXX Europe 600, 2005-07 to 2025-12: Sharpe 0.27 after costs,
  strategy card "no evidence" (t 1.30, timing placebo p 0.69). So two of the three equity markets here have been seen
  with this exact design.
- H11 (2026-10-09): a 4/48-day and 252-day trend rule, as signs, on the S&P 500 and on EUR, JPY, GBP, CHF, AUD and CAD
  against the dollar (with carry), 2000-2025 (research/reports/H11.md). So the currencies have been seen with another
  trend rule.
- The 5- and 10-year Treasury series, crude oil and the Russell 2000 have not been looked at in this project. Only the
  first dates of the free series were read (2026-10-20): IEI from 2007-01, IEF 2002-07, USO 2006-04, the currencies
  2003-12.
- Martin's settings were chosen on 20+ years of futures across asset classes, bonds and crude included, ending around
  2012-2020. The first half of the window is partly in-sample for the design.

MARKET BEHAVIOUR AND MECHANISM
Time-series momentum across asset classes (Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen 2012; Hurst, Ooi and Pedersen 2017): prices
that have risen for weeks to months keep rising, because news is absorbed slowly and investors adjust gradually.
Treasuries, oil and currencies trend for different reasons (policy cycles, supply shocks, rate differentials), so the
markets are less correlated than three equity indices, and a book of them has more independent bets than H13's.

MARKETS (12; the tradable future, and the research series that stands in for its history)
- Equity: Micro E-mini S&P 500 (S&P 500 index), Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100 (Nasdaq-100), Micro E-mini Russell 2000
  (Russell 2000), each plus a flat dividend yield (1.9%, 0.9%, 1.3%) minus the US 3-month rate.
- Treasuries: 5-year note ZF (IEI, iShares 3-7 year, total return), 10-year note ZN (IEF, iShares 7-10 year, total
  return), each minus the US 3-month rate.
- Energy: Micro WTI crude MCL (USO, total return, minus the US 3-month rate: the fund holds front-month WTI futures and
  pays the roll, less a 0.7% fee).
- Currencies against the dollar: Micro EUR, GBP, AUD, JPY, CHF and CAD futures (Norges Bank daily fixings crossed to
  US dollars, plus the currency's 3-month rate minus the US one, the future's carry).
- Rates: OECD 3-month interbank rates, monthly, used from the following month on.

DATA AND WINDOW
- Daily bars from Yahoo (equity indices, ETFs) and Norges Bank (currencies) through the lab catalog (instrument master config/instruments.toml: idx:SPX, idx:NDX, idx:RUT,
  etf:IEI, etf:IEF, etf:USO, fxusd:EUR, fxusd:GBP, fxusd:AUD, fxusd:JPY, fxusd:CHF, fxusd:CAD).
- Window: 2007-01-01 to 2025-12-31, all twelve series from the start (IEI starts 2007-01). Each market is flat until its
  signal and volatility are defined, so the first months are mostly warm-up. 2026 stays frozen. Halves: 2007-01-01 to
  2016-06-30 and 2016-07-01 to 2025-12-31.

SIGNAL, POSITIONS, COSTS (research/strategies/multi_asset_trend.toml, version 1)
- H13's P: volatility-normalised EMA differences at (10, 20), (20, 40), (40, 80) days through the compound sigmoid,
  averaged over the three speeds (quantlab's trend_forecast, the same code the paper book runs).
- Each market sized to 10% a year of volatility (exponential average, span 20), equal weights (1/12 each), position
  capped at 1 x equity per market. Decided at the close of t, traded at the close of t+1. Band 2% of equity.
- Costs per side from the instrument master: 1.5 bps the S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100, 3 bps the Russell 2000, 1 bp Treasuries and currencies, 2 bps crude; 4
  rolls a year at 1 bp.

PRIMARY OUTCOME AND PASS CRITERIA (all three)
The book's strategy card (quantlab.validation, standards in validation/standards/strategy.toml) over the window:
1. Verdict "evidence of an edge": significant after the trial count, and its timing beats the timing placebo.
2. Sharpe after costs positive in both halves.
3. Sharpe still positive at twice the costs.
KILL IF the card says "no evidence" and the Sharpe after costs in the second half is zero or negative.
Otherwise: inconclusive.

SECONDARY OUTCOMES (reported, not tested)
Per asset class (equity, Treasuries, crude, currencies): Sharpe, worst drawdown, correlation with the S&P 500; the
book's power to detect its own shrunk Sharpe; the signal card at 5 and 20 days.

TRIALS
3 confirmatory: the book's strategy card and the signal card at two horizons. 4 per-class breakdowns, descriptive.
No parameter is varied.

WHAT EACH RESULT LEADS TO
The owner asked for the paper book either way: config/portfolio.multi_asset_trend_paper.toml runs P on the 12 futures
at 10,000,000 NOK of pretend capital, and its header states this verdict. Pass: the paper record tests a model that
passed. Inconclusive or kill: it tests the setup and how the design behaves. Never live (employer rule).

KNOWN RISKS AND BIASES
- Stand-ins: ETFs and spot rates are not the futures. IEI and IEF have somewhat different durations from the 5- and
  10-year notes; USO changed how it rolls in 2020; currency carry uses monthly interbank rates.
- Twelve markets, six of them currencies: equal weights make half the book currencies.
- Contract rounding: at 10,000,000 NOK a Treasury sleeve holds one to three contracts, so paper positions are coarse.
- Contract specs for ZF, ZN and MCL were written from memory (2026-10-20) and are marked unconfirmed in the table.
- Costs are assumptions, not measurements.
