PRE-REGISTRATION H2: LIQUIDATION OVERSHOOT -> REVERSAL (PROXY VERSION)
Version 1. Written 2026-09-28, before any H2 backtest was run. Changes after results = a new version, logged as extra trials.

DATA LIMITATION (read first)
No exchange publishes liquidation history, and only a few days have been recorded (the recorder was restarted
2026-09-28 and keeps running). This version therefore tests a PROXY event (open-interest drop + extreme return +
volume spike) and is labelled PROXY in every output. It tests "forced-deleveraging-shaped hours revert", not
liquidations directly. It must be validated against recorded liquidations once months exist: do proxy events coincide
with liquidation notional > 5x its median?

MECHANISM
Forced liquidations are price-insensitive market orders. In thin books they push price past where discretionary
traders would clear it; once the forced flow ends, price partially reverts.

EXPECTED SIGN
After a long-liquidation cascade, 12-48h returns beat a volatility-matched baseline; after a short squeeze, the
mirror (returns below baseline). Stronger for endogenous cascades than for news-driven ones.

DATA
- Binance USD-M perp 1h candles (OHLCV) for BTC, ETH, SOL.
- Binance 5-minute open interest in coins (lagged 5 minutes): from 2021-11 (BTC), 2021-12 (ETH), SOL as available.
- Development window: OI start -> 2025-12-31 (trade exits must be before 2026-01-01).

EVENT DEFINITION (hour t, evaluated at its close)
- sigma(t): std of hourly log close-to-close returns over the 720 bars before t (30 days).
- ret(t) = ln(close_t / close_{t-1}).
- dOI(t) = OI at t's close / OI at the previous close - 1 (as-of joins on availability).
- volume spike: volume_t > 5 * median(volume over the 720 bars before t).
- Long-liquidation cascade: ret < -3 sigma AND dOI < -3% AND volume spike.  Direction of the trade: +1.
- Short squeeze: ret > +3 sigma AND dOI < -3% AND volume spike.            Direction of the trade: -1.
- An event within 48h after an earlier event of the same coin and direction is part of the same cascade (merged).
- LOOSE variant (registered now; run only if the primary definition has fewer than 30 pooled development events):
  2.5 sigma, dOI < -2%, volume > 3x median. Its trials are logged separately.

ENTRY / EXIT
- Exhaustion: the first bar k in t+1 .. t+6 with volume_k < 2 * median volume; entry at the open of k+1. If none,
  entry at the open of t+7. (Proxy for "liquidations fall back below 2x median".)
- Hold h in {12, 24, 48} hours; exit at the open h bars after entry.
- Stop: long below the cascade low (min low from t to k); short above the cascade high. Checked intrabar on the
  high/low; a bar opening through the stop fills at its open.

EVENT STUDY
- CAR_h = direction * ln(open(entry + h) / open(entry)) (no stop), h in {12, 24, 48}.
- Baseline: for each event, 20 random hours of the same coin, not within 48h of any event, whose trailing 24h realized
  vol at entry is within +-20% of the event's; their forward returns are multiplied by the event's direction.
- Abnormal return = CAR_h - baseline mean. Mean and 95% bootstrap CI over events (clusters are already merged, so
  events are resampled individually).
- Placebo: 1000 sets of random vol-matched pseudo-events (same coins, directions and count); p = share of placebo
  mean CARs >= the real one.
- Pooled over coins and both directions (sign-adjusted); per-direction and per-coin breakdowns reported.
- News split: an event is news-driven if its hour is within [-2h, +4h] of a scheduled US CPI release (08:30 New York
  time) or FOMC statement (14:00 New York time) on the official calendars (Fed, BLS). If the calendars can't be
  obtained, the split is not run and that is stated.

STRATEGY
- Trades as above with the stop; taker both sides on Kraken perp, spread x3 (stressed) and volatility-scaled; funding
  from Binance settlements. Also at 2x these stressed costs.

PARAMETER GRID (3 configurations; +3 only if LOOSE is triggered)
hold in {12, 24, 48}.

PASS CRITERIA
- Mean abnormal return > 0 with the bootstrap CI excluding 0, and beats placebo at 95% (p < 0.05).
- Net trade returns positive at stressed costs and at 2x stressed costs.
- Positive in at least 2 of 3 coins; positive in most years; deflated Sharpe (H2 family) of trade returns >= 0.95.
- Same sign in the holdout (run once, after approval).
- Plus, before any deployment: validation of the proxy against recorded liquidations.

KILL IF
- CAR is not distinguishable from the matched baseline, or the edge is gone at 2x stressed costs.
