PRE-REGISTRATION H4: DOWNSIDE PROTECTION BOUGHT AS KALSHI "NO" CONTRACTS IS OVERPRICED
Version 1. Written 2026-10-03, before any H4 test was run. Changes after results = a new version (lock it again), logged
as extra trials.

PRIOR EXPOSURE
- notebooks/prediction_markets.ipynb, run 2026-10-03: one live snapshot of Kalshi daily quotes, and a table of how a
  perp hedges a binary. No history of Kalshi's daily markets has been looked at.
- H3 (research log 2026-09-28): BTC option buyers paid about 6 volatility points more than was realized. Insurance on
  BTC has been expensive in the option market.

MARKET BEHAVIOUR
A "No" contract on "BTC above K" with K just below the price pays 1 if BTC settles below K: a digital put, i.e.
protection for a long position. Bought at the ask plus the fee, it loses money on average.

MECHANISM
Insurance against a fall is in demand and its sellers are paid for carrying it (the same premium H3 measured in
options). On Kalshi the buyer also pays the spread and a fee. The practical question behind this: is "perp long plus
Kalshi No contracts" a sensible capped-loss position, and what does the cap cost?

OBSERVABLE PREDICTION AND EXPECTED SIGN
Mean profit per contract (payoff - cost) of buying this protection is below 0. Its cost exceeds the breach rate.

DATA
- Deribit: BTC option chain snapshots recorded about hourly by this project's collector (data/options/deribit/BTC; they
  exist from 2026-09-26, with gaps whenever the recording machine slept). Each snapshot carries Deribit's index price.
- Kalshi (public API): the daily "Bitcoin price at 5 PM New York" event, series KXBTCD, one event per day (ticker
  KXBTCD-<yy><MON><dd>17), levels every 250 USD. Quote at a snapshot = the latest 1-minute candle's Yes bid and ask
  within the 10 minutes before the snapshot; markets without a two-sided quote in that window are left out. Outcome
  and settlement value from Kalshi after settlement.
- Rows: every (snapshot, level) with the level within +-3% of Deribit's index (|ln(K/S)| <= 0.03) and 1 to 26 hours
  left to settlement. Built by src/research/hypotheses/binary_panel.py (build_panel).
- PILOT window: snapshots before 2026-10-04 00:00 UTC (already recorded when this was written). It checks that the
  pipeline works. Its numbers are reported and labelled pilot; no conclusion is drawn from them and no rule below is
  changed because of them (a change = a new version).
- CONFIRMATORY window: snapshots from 2026-10-05 00:00 UTC to 2026-11-16 00:00 UTC. None of it existed when this was
  written. It is run once, after 2026-11-17, when every market in it has settled. If it holds fewer than 20 daily
  events with rows (the recorder sleeps with the laptop), the end date moves forward day by day until it holds 20.
- This is stricter than the project's frozen-holdout rule (2026 price history is frozen for strategy research): the
  test data are in the future.

INFERENCE
Rows overlap heavily (one event at many snapshots and many levels share one outcome). Every interval is a bootstrap
over whole events (2000 resamples, 95%); rows are never treated as independent.

SIGNAL DEFINITION (the protection bought; at each snapshot time t)
- Rows with the level 0.5% to 2% below Deribit's index (-0.02 <= ln(K/S) <= -0.005) and at least 2 hours to settlement.
- cost = No ask + fee, No ask = 1 - Yes bid, fee = 0.07 x No ask x (1 - No ask) per contract.
- payoff = 1 if the market settled No (BTC below the level), else 0. Held to settlement.

PRIMARY OUTCOME
Mean of (payoff - cost) per contract over all settled rows of the confirmatory window, with its event-bootstrap 95%
interval.

SECONDARY OUTCOMES
- Breach rate (share of rows that paid) next to the mean cost.
- Richness: cost - (1 - implied), implied = the Deribit smile's probability of settling above the level (as in H5).
- cover_bps: the cost, in basis points of a long position's size, of holding (S - K) / S contracts per unit of
  position, i.e. enough to be paid the whole loss down to K if it is breached.
- The Deribit put spread around the same level at the nearest expiry (within 14 hours), ask of the upper put minus bid
  of the lower, per unit of payout, next to Kalshi's cost on the same rows. It is a ramp at another expiry, so it is
  context, not a like-for-like price.

PARAMETER GRID (1 configuration)
The moneyness band, the 2-hour minimum and the fee are fixed above. Nothing else is run.

PASS CRITERIA (confirmatory window, at least 20 events)
Mean (payoff - cost) < 0 with the interval excluding 0: the protection is overpriced, and selling it, not buying it, is
the side with the premium.

KILL IF
The interval includes 0 (not distinguishable from fairly priced) or lies above 0 (cheap protection: then the
capped-loss position is worth costing properly against options).

KNOWN RISKS AND BIASES
- A premium for sellers is paid for rare large losses; six weeks can show a premium only because no crash happened.
- About 40 events; rows within an event are one outcome. Snapshots cluster in the hours the laptop is awake.
- Buying at the ask is assumed fillable at the quoted size; sizes are not checked.
- Different settlement index than the perp being protected (Kalshi: BRTI; the perp: Kraken's mark).
- Kalshi's fee rate is from its general schedule. Whether this venue may be used at all is not addressed.
