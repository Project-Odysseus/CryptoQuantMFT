PRE-REGISTRATION H9: A RANK BUFFER CUTS THE TAKER BASKET'S TURNOVER WITHOUT COSTING IT RETURN
Version 1. Written 2026-10-05, before any H9 test was run. Changes after results = a new version (lock it again), logged
as extra trials.

PRIOR EXPOSURE (what has already been looked at that bears on this)
- The taker-buy basket itself has been studied on all of this data: 2026-09-26 (8 features, two universes), 2026-09-26
  robustness (45 configurations of universe size, leg size and rebalance interval), 2026-09-29 (on Kraken prices
  through the engine), 2026-10-03 (its two legs), 2026-10-04 (signal lab, H6 comparison: Sharpe 1.08 on the
  point-in-time universe, 2022-03 to 2025).
- No version with a buffer, or any other change to how coins enter and leave, has been run.
- So the basket's level of return is known and is not what is tested. What is tested is the difference a buffer makes
  on the same data, which has not been seen. A relative result on data the base rule was chosen on is weaker than a
  test on new data: the paper book is where it is confirmed.

MARKET BEHAVIOUR
The basket's signal (the share of a coin's volume that was aggressive buying over 7 days) changes slowly. A coin that
slips from just inside the top fifth to just outside it has barely changed, yet the basket sells it and buys its
neighbour.

MECHANISM
Trades at the edge of the ranking carry almost no information and cost a full round trip. Holding a coin until it has
clearly left the group keeps the positions that still have the property and skips the trades that don't change it.

OBSERVABLE PREDICTION AND EXPECTED SIGN
With a buffer the book trades clearly less, pays less in costs, and its return before costs falls by less than the
costs saved: net return and Sharpe are not lower.

DATA
The signal lab's data and costs (src/research/signal_lab.py): Binance USDT-perp daily closes, volume, taker-buy volume
and settled funding; the 30 most traded coins whose Kraken perp had opened by that day (point-in-time listing
snapshot); 0.05% fee and slippage by liquidity tier per side; funding on every position. Window: the first day with
at least 10 names, to 2025-12-31. The frozen holdout (2026 on) stays locked.

RULE
Score: taker-buy share of the last 7 days' volume. Every 10 days (counted from the window's first day):
- Baseline (the rule in the paper book): long the top 20% of the universe by score, short the bottom 20%.
- Buffered: a coin enters the long side when it is in the top 20%, and stays until it is no longer in the top 35% (or
  leaves the universe). The short side mirrors it: enters in the bottom 20%, stays while in the bottom 35%.
Both: equal weight within a side, half of gross long and half short, 1x gross, flat with fewer than 10 names in the
universe. Between rebalance days the weights are held; a position is re-trimmed only when it has drifted more than 1%
of equity from its weight (the paper book's band). Decided and traded at the day's close, as in the earlier studies.

PRIMARY OUTCOME
Two numbers, buffered against baseline over the whole window at 1x costs: turnover a year, and net return a year.

SECONDARY OUTCOMES
- Sharpe, worst drawdown, costs and funding a year, average names held per side, for both.
- The difference in daily net returns, annualised, with a 95% interval (circular block bootstrap, 20-day blocks,
  2,000 draws, seed 0).
- Per calendar year, both.
- The same comparison on today's Kraken list from 2020-06 (the universe of the original study). Reported, not judged.

PARAMETER GRID (every configuration is a trial; count them)
5 trials: the baseline as defined here (its 1%-band variant has not been run before); buffered with exit at 35%
(primary); buffered with exit at 30% and at 45%; buffered 35% at 2x costs (with the baseline at 2x costs as its
comparison, not counted). The today's-list comparison adds 2: 7 in total. Nothing else is run.

PASS CRITERIA (all three)
1. Turnover is at least 25% lower than the baseline's.
2. Net return a year is not more than 1 percentage point below the baseline's.
3. At 2x costs the buffered book's net return is above the baseline's.

KILL IF (any)
- Turnover is not lower.
- Net return is more than 3 percentage points a year below the baseline's.
Between kill and pass: inconclusive, and the paper book keeps its rule.

WHAT A PASS LEADS TO
The buffer becomes an option of the basket (`exit_quantile`), switched on in the multi-strategy paper book only, at
the primary width. The paper book then shows whether turnover really falls.

KNOWN RISKS AND BIASES
- The base rule was selected on this data, so its return here is flattered; only the difference is new.
- A buffer holds more names per side on average, so each position is smaller and the book is slightly more diversified:
  part of any change in volatility comes from that, not from better selection.
- Binance prices and funding stand in for Kraken's; delisted Kraken perps are missing.
- One window of under four years.
