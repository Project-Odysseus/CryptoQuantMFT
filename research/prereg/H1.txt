PRE-REGISTRATION H1: LEVERAGE CROWDING (FUNDING + BASIS)
Version 1. Written 2026-09-28, before any H1 backtest was run. Changes after results = a new version, logged as extra trials.

PRIOR EXPOSURE (disclosed)
- The development window has already been looked at for a related signal: docs/research_log.md (2026-09-26 and
  2026-09-27) measured a 30-day funding z-score (IC -0.047 at 1 day, t -2.1, BTC/ETH 2020-2025), a crowding composite,
  and always-on / filtered carry on BTC and ETH. This study is therefore partly confirmatory, not independent.
  Its deflated Sharpe is reported against both the H1 trial count and the global ledger count (which includes those
  studies).

MECHANISM
Funding and basis are the price of leveraged long exposure. Extreme values mean crowded, fragile long positioning;
suppliers of leverage (shorts, carry traders) earn a premium for carrying that crash risk (Schmeling, Schrimpf &
Todorov, BIS "Crypto Carry"). Forced deleveraging of crowded longs pushes subsequent returns down.

EXPECTED SIGN
- High funding z  -> lower forward 1-7d returns and a fatter left tail; strongest when open interest is rising.
- Very negative funding z -> higher forward returns (weaker effect).
- Carry (long spot / short perp) earns positive net returns when funding clears the cost hurdle.

DATA (point-in-time; research/../src/research/pit.py)
- Price: Binance USD-M perp 1h candles (BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT); Binance spot 1h for the carry spot leg.
- Funding: Binance and Bybit settled rates at their actual settlement timestamps.
- Open interest: Binance 5-minute (from 2021-11/12), Bybit hourly (from 2020); each lagged one sampling interval.
- Basis: nearest Binance USDT quarterly with >= 14 days to expiry, annualised vs Binance spot (BTC, ETH only).
- Development window: first decision date with 90 days of signal history -> 2025-12-31. Holdout (2026-01-01 on) is
  locked and is run once, at the end, only with the owner's approval.

SIGNAL DEFINITIONS (decision time T = 00:00 UTC daily; fills at the open of the hourly bar starting at T)
- Per venue v: F_v(T) = mean over settlements s in (T-3d, T] of rate_s * 8h / interval_s, where interval_s is the
  time since the previous settlement on that venue (normalises 4h/8h intervals to an 8h-equivalent rate).
- Weights: open interest in USD at T (Binance oi_usd; Bybit oi * Binance perp close at T). If either venue's OI is
  unavailable at T, venues with funding get equal weight.
- F(T) = sum_v w_v F_v(T). Annualised: F_ann = F * 3 * 365.
- z(T) = (F(T) - mean(F over the 90 daily values ending at T)) / std(same); at least 60 values required.
- dOI3(T) = OI_coins(T) / OI_coins(T-3d) - 1, OI_coins = Binance OI + Bybit OI in coins (only venues present at both
  times). "OI rising" = dOI3 > 0.
- Secondary: basis z(T), the same 90-day z-score of the annualised quarterly basis (BTC, ETH).
- Forward return: r_h(T) = ln(open(T + h days) / open(T)), h in {1, 3, 7}.

PREDICTIVE TESTS
1. Pooled regression r_h ~ z, Driscoll-Kraay SEs with lags = h (overlap and cross-coin correlation). Expected slope < 0.
   Per-coin Newey-West regressions (lags = h).
2. Interaction: r_h ~ z + 1[dOI3>0] + z*1[dOI3>0]. Expected interaction < 0.
3. Quintile sorts of z pooled over coins (breakpoints from the pooled development sample): count, mean, skew and 5%
   quantile of r_h per quintile. Expected: Q5 mean < Q1 mean and Q5 5% quantile < Q1 5% quantile.
4. Same sorts and regression with basis z (secondary measure).

RULES (only these two)
(a) Directional tilt on a long-biased perp position.
    Base weight 0.5. Crowded (z > 2 AND dOI3 > 0) -> weight 0.0. Washed out (z < -1.5) -> weight 1.0. Otherwise 0.5.
    After a trigger the state is kept for at least `min_hold` days unless the opposite trigger fires.
    The tested object is the OVERLAY (weight - 0.5, i.e. -0.5 / 0 / +0.5); base+overlay vs base is also reported.
    Venue costs: Kraken perp taker, spread and slippage scaled with volatility (src/research/costs.py); funding charged
    from Binance settlements (Kraken funding history does not cover the development window).
(b) Delta-neutral carry: long spot, short perp, per unit notional.
    Round trip RT = 2 * (spot cost per side + perp cost per side) in a calm market (4 legs).
    hurdle_ann = RT * 365 / expected_hold_days. Enter when F_ann(T) > hurdle_ann; exit when F_ann(T) < 0.5 * hurdle_ann.
    P&L: spot return - perp return + Binance funding received - costs (4 legs, volatility-scaled).
    Return on capital = return on notional / 1.5 (spot plus 2x perp margin), reported alongside.

PARAMETER GRID (12 configurations in total; nothing else will be tried under version 1)
- Tilt: signal in {funding z, basis z (BTC/ETH only)} x min_hold in {1, 3, 7} days             -> 6
- Carry: expected_hold_days in {7, 14, 30} x fees in {Binance spot+perp, Kraken spot+perp}    -> 6
  (Kraken fees are applied to Binance prices and funding, a cost-sensitivity case.)

ROBUSTNESS
- Every configuration: gross, net, and net at 2x costs. Per-coin and per-year tables.
- Placebo (tilt): circular shift of each coin's signal inputs by a random 60+ days, 500 runs; share of placebo pooled
  Sharpes below the real one.
- Placebo (carry): the same shift of the in/out state against funding; always-on carry reported as the natural benchmark.
- Deflated Sharpe with the H1 trial count and the global ledger count.

PASS CRITERIA (all required; per rule)
- Net development Sharpe > 0 and deflated Sharpe (H1 family) >= 0.95.
- Net Sharpe > 0 at 2x costs.
- Positive net Sharpe in at least 2 of 3 coins (2 of 2 for basis-based variants).
- Positive in most development years (>= 60% of calendar years).
- Beats placebo at 95%.
- Same sign in the holdout (run once, after approval).

KILL IF
- The pooled z slope is not negative in at least 2 of 3 coins at h = 3 days, or
- Carry does not clear its 4 legs of costs at the Binance fee level (net <= 0 for all expected_hold values).

ADDENDUM 1 (2026-09-28, data cleaning, written before any H1 result was computed)
- Open-interest rows <= 0 are treated as missing (Binance's 5-minute archive has whole days of zeros, e.g. 2022-03-07/08
  and 2024-07-10..13). An OI value older than 1 day at T counts as missing.
- Bybit's early OI (2020-2021) shows large swings (e.g. tripling on 2021-05-19) that can't be verified. Not filtered;
  reported as a limitation.

ADDENDUM 2 (2026-09-28, holdout evaluation plan, written after owner approval and BEFORE the holdout was opened)
- One look, one configuration: rule (b) carry, Binance fees, expected_hold_days = 14 (the pre-registered middle
  configuration). No other H1 configuration is evaluated on the holdout.
- The state machine runs continuously through the development data into the holdout (a position open on
  2025-12-31 carries over), exactly as a live sleeve would. Only days from 2026-01-01 are scored.
- Holdout window: 2026-01-01 to the last full month in the Binance monthly archive (2026-08-31).
- PASS if the pooled (equal-weight BTC/ETH/SOL) net return on notional over the holdout is > 0. Also reported,
  not part of the pass rule: net at 2x costs, per coin, time in position, entries, funding vs costs, always-on carry.
- Whatever the outcome, this configuration is not re-tuned on the holdout. A changed rule goes to paper trading.
