PRE-REGISTRATION H8: PAIRS OF KRAKEN-TRADABLE PERPS THAT MOVED TOGETHER COME BACK TOGETHER
Version 1. Written 2026-10-04, before any H8 test was run. Changes after results = a new version (lock it again), logged
as extra trials.

PRIOR EXPOSURE (what has already been looked at that bears on this)
- No pairs or spread study has been run in this project.
- Related, on the same coins: the cross-sectional study (2026-09-26) found a 1-day reversal in the rankings (IC -0.03
  to -0.04) that daily trading costs wiped out. That is a different rule (ranks over all coins, no pair selection, no
  entry threshold), but it says short-horizon reversal exists and is expensive to collect.
- The regime study (2026-10-04) found the book's trend rules lose in flat markets. That is the reason for looking at
  a spread trade; it used none of the data in this form.

MARKET BEHAVIOUR
Two liquid coins whose prices tracked each other closely over three months, and then drift apart, tend to come back
together.

MECHANISM
Most of a coin's move is the market's. When two close substitutes diverge without news that separates them, it is
usually one-sided flow in one of them (a liquidation, a large order), and the price impact fades. The other side is
whoever needed to trade at once. The risk is that the divergence is news and never closes.

OBSERVABLE PREDICTION AND EXPECTED SIGN
Short the coin that ran ahead and long the one that lagged, and the position earns as the gap closes: a positive net
return that does not depend on the market's direction.

DATA (point-in-time)
- Binance USDT-margined perpetual daily closes and settled funding (src/data/binance_archive.py), standing in for
  Kraken's.
- Coins: at each formation date, the 10 with the highest 30-day average quote volume among those whose Kraken perp
  had opened by then (research/data/kraken_perp_listings.csv) and that have a close on every day of the formation
  window.
- Window: trading periods from the first date with 10 such coins to 2025-12-31. The holdout (2026 on) stays locked.

RULE (the distance method of Gatev, Goetzmann and Rouwenhorst, with one day's delay)
- Every 30 days: over the previous 90 days (formation), scale each coin's closes to start at 1. For each of the 45
  pairs, distance = the sum of squared differences between the two scaled series. Take the 5 pairs with the smallest
  distance. sigma = the standard deviation of that pair's formation difference.
- Over the next 30 days (trading), scale both closes to 1 at the last formation close. spread = scaled A - scaled B.
- Open when |spread| > 2 sigma at a close: short the higher coin, long the lower, equal dollar amounts.
  Close when the spread has crossed zero at a close, or on the period's last day. A pair can open again in the same
  period.
- Every decision is traded at the NEXT day's close (one day's delay), so no trade uses the close that produced it.
- Size: each open pair holds 10% of equity long and 10% short. With all 5 open the book is 1x gross, dollar-neutral.

COSTS
0.05% taker fee per side, slippage by liquidity tier (3 to 25 bps, slippage_by_liquidity), settled funding on every
position, through src/research/portfolio.simulate_portfolio (which also charges the daily re-weighting).

PRIMARY OUTCOME
The annualised Sharpe ratio of the book's daily net returns over the whole window at 1x costs, for the rule above.

SECONDARY OUTCOMES
- Return a year on equity, worst drawdown, average gross exposure, number of trades, share closed by convergence,
  average days held.
- 95% interval for the Sharpe (circular block bootstrap, 20-day blocks, 2,000 draws, seed 0).
- Per calendar year. Return split into price, funding and costs.
- Return on flat, middling and trending days (the regime study's label), and correlation with the taker basket on
  the top-50 point-in-time universe and with BTC.
- The same rule traded at the signal's own close (no delay), to show what the delay costs. Reported, not judged.

PARAMETER GRID (every configuration is a trial; count them)
10 trials, nothing else is run:
- 9 = formation days {60, 90, 120} x entry threshold {1.5, 2.0, 2.5} sigma, at 1x costs. Primary: 90 days, 2.0.
- 1 = the primary at 2x costs.
The no-delay variant is an 11th configuration and is logged as a trial too: 11 in total.

PASS CRITERIA (all five)
1. Primary Sharpe >= 0.7.
2. The lower bound of its bootstrap interval is above 0.
3. Net return at 2x costs is positive.
4. At least 7 of the 9 grid cells have a positive Sharpe.
5. At least 3 of the calendar years 2022 to 2025 are positive (2022 counts from the window's first day).

KILL IF (any)
- Primary Sharpe < 0.3.
- Net return at 2x costs is negative.
Between kill and pass: inconclusive, and the sleeve is not built.

WHAT A PASS LEADS TO
A candidate for a paper book only. The runtime has no pairs sleeve yet, so a pass means building one and running it
in paper for 13 weeks before any other decision. Not live.

KNOWN RISKS AND BIASES
- Binance prices and funding are proxies for Kraken's; perps Kraken delisted are missing from the listing snapshot.
- Under four years of data and about 45 trading periods: low power.
- Daily closes only. Divergences that open and close within a day are invisible, and the one-day delay gives up part
  of any fast reversion.
- A pair that diverges on real news keeps diverging until the period ends: losses are larger than typical gains.
- Four legs per round trip on coins with 3 to 15 bps of assumed slippage.
