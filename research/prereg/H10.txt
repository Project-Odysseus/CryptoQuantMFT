PRE-REGISTRATION H10: THE US MORNING'S MOVE IN BTC AND ETH CONTINUES INTO THE US AFTERNOON
Version 1. Written 2026-10-06, before any H10 test was run. Changes after results = a new version (lock it again), logged
as extra trials.

PRIOR EXPOSURE (what has already been looked at that bears on this)
- 2026-09-26, intraday study (15m and 1h bars, BTC and ETH, all hours pooled): the last 4 hours' move predicted the
  next 4 hours with a *negative* rank correlation (-0.03 to -0.046, same sign in 6-7 of 7 years), worth a few basis
  points. That is reversal, measured over every hour of the day, not conditioned on the session. It points against
  this hypothesis and is the reason the result is reported in both directions.
- The same study: the 20:00-22:00 UTC hours were up a few basis points on average (a lead, 48 hour-coin cells tried).
- 2026-10-06, volatility clock: the hours 13:00-16:00 UTC are the most volatile of the day, increasingly so since
  2022. No return was looked at.
- No test has conditioned a later return on the US morning's return.

MARKET BEHAVIOUR
In stocks the first half-hour's return predicts the last half-hour's (Gao, Han, Li and Zhou, 2018). Since US-listed
bitcoin funds and macro news moved crypto's activity into US hours, the same may hold for BTC and ETH: the direction
set in the first two hours after the US stock open carries through the rest of the US trading day.

MECHANISM
Large investors work orders through the day, and funds that must trade at the close do so in the direction of the
day's flow. News that arrives around the open is absorbed over hours, not minutes. The other side is whoever
provides liquidity intraday and is paid the spread and fees for it.

OBSERVABLE PREDICTION AND EXPECTED SIGN
On US weekdays, the return from 11:35 to 16:00 New York time has the same sign as the return from 09:30 to 11:30:
positive mean of sign(morning) x afternoon return.

DATA
Binance USDT-perp 5-minute bars for BTC and ETH (src/research/pit.py), 2021-03-01 to 2025-12-31. Times are New York
local time (so the US open stays at 09:30 through daylight saving). Weekdays only; US holidays are not removed.
The frozen holdout (2026 on) stays locked.

RULE
- Signal: the log return from the open of the 09:30 bar to the close of the bar ending 11:30.
- Position: long if it is positive, short if negative (flat if exactly zero), entered at the close of the bar ending
  11:35 (five minutes after the signal is known) and closed at the close of the bar ending 16:00.
- One trade per coin per weekday. BTC and ETH each get half of the capital.

COSTS
Round trip per trade: 11 basis points (Kraken's 0.05% taker fee each way plus 0.5 bp of spread each way, the spread
from the first Kraken measurements). A maker case at 4 basis points (0.02% each way, no spread) is reported and is
optimistic: a resting order is filled more often when the price is moving against it.

PRIMARY OUTCOME
The mean return per trade of the rule, net of 11 basis points, BTC and ETH pooled, over the whole window, with a 95%
interval from a circular block bootstrap of the daily series (20-day blocks, 2,000 draws, seed 0).

SECONDARY OUTCOMES
- The gross mean per trade, and the net mean at 4 basis points.
- Per coin, per year, per weekday; first half (to 2023-06-30) and second half.
- The slope of the afternoon return on the morning return (Newey-West, 5 lags).
- The rule only on days when the morning move is larger than its trailing 60-day median (known at 11:30).
- The mirror rule (fade the morning), gross and net: the earlier study suggests reversal.

PARAMETER GRID (every configuration is a trial; count them)
6 trials, nothing else is run: signal window {first hour 09:30-10:30, first two hours 09:30-11:30} x exit {16:00, 20:00
New York}, at 11 bp (4 cells; primary: two hours, 16:00); the primary at 4 bp; the primary on large-morning days only.
The mirror rule is the same trades with the sign flipped and is not counted separately.

PASS CRITERIA (all four)
1. Net mean per trade at 11 bp is positive and its 95% interval is above 0.
2. Positive net at 11 bp in both halves of the window.
3. Positive net at 11 bp for BTC and for ETH separately.
4. At least 3 of the 4 grid cells are positive net at 11 bp.

KILL IF
The gross mean per trade (before any cost) is below 5.5 basis points: then not even a maker on both legs with no
adverse fills would keep half of it.
Between kill and pass: inconclusive, and nothing is built.

WHAT A PASS LEADS TO
A candidate for a paper sleeve. The runtime would need an intraday decision clock in New York time first.

KNOWN RISKS AND BIASES
- The move this tries to capture is a few basis points in the literature; fees of 11 make a pass unlikely.
- Our own earlier evidence at this horizon is reversal, not continuation.
- US holidays are in the sample as ordinary weekdays (a small dilution).
- Binance prices stand in for Kraken's. Funding over a 4.5-hour hold is ignored (about 0.2 bp at typical rates).
- The mirror rule's result, if it looks good, is a lead from this data and needs its own test.
