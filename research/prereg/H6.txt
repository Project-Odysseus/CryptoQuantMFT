PRE-REGISTRATION H6: CROSS-SECTIONAL FUNDING CARRY ON KRAKEN-TRADABLE PERPS
Version 1. Written 2026-10-04, before any H6 test was run. Changes after results = a new version (lock it again), logged
as extra trials.

PRIOR EXPOSURE (what has already been looked at that bears on this)
This signal on this data has been looked at three times, so nothing in the development window is unseen:
- 2026-09-26, cross_sectional_study.py: funding_7d long/short, weekly. Full Binance universe: Sharpe 1.24 to 2023,
  -0.70 from 2024 to 2026-08. Coins Kraken lists today, top 50: 1.19 and 0.42. That holdout ran into 2026, so the
  frozen window has been seen in aggregate up to 2026-08.
- 2026-10-04, signal lab: the same rule on today's Kraken list. Top 50: 1.09 to 2023, 0.93 for 2024-2025. Top 30: 1.77
  and 1.63. Chosen next to the taker basket by an in-sample rule.
- 2026-09-28, H1b: timed spot-against-perp carry on BTC, ETH, SOL. Failed its holdout look.
Consequence: this test cannot confirm the signal. It can only reject it, by showing that the earlier result does not
survive a point-in-time coin list, doubled costs and nearby definitions. Confirmation, if it passes, is prospective
(see PROSPECTIVE PHASE).

MARKET BEHAVIOUR
Coins whose perpetual charges longs the most funding do not outperform by enough to pay for it. Short the coins with
the highest funding and long the ones with the lowest, and the book collects the funding difference.

MECHANISM
Funding is the price of leveraged long exposure. Leveraged buyers crowd into the popular coins and pay; whoever takes
the other side is paid for carrying squeeze risk on crowded coins and for holding unloved ones. The other side of
this trade is the leveraged long.

OBSERVABLE PREDICTION AND EXPECTED SIGN
A dollar-neutral book, long low funding and short high funding, has a positive net return. Funding received is
positive and is the main source; the price legs are near zero.

DATA (point-in-time)
- Binance USDT-margined perpetuals, daily klines and settled funding, delisted symbols included
  (src/data/binance_archive.py). Binance prices and funding stand in for Kraken's: Kraken's public funding history
  covers about one year only.
- Universe each day: the 50 symbols with the highest 30-day average quote volume that have traded at least 60 days
  (src/research/portfolio.liquid_universe), then only those whose Kraken perpetual had opened before that day
  (research/data/kraken_perp_listings.csv, a snapshot of Kraken's openingDate per perp taken 2026-10-04).
- Window: the first day with at least 10 names in that universe, to 2025-12-31. Kraken's linear perps began
  2022-03-22, so 2020 and 2021 are out. The holdout (2026-01-01 on) stays locked.

SIGNAL (known at each day's close; traded at that close, as in the earlier studies)
score = minus the mean of the last 7 daily funding sums (at least 5). Long the top 20% of the universe by score,
short the bottom 20%, equal weight within each side, 1x gross, dollar-neutral. Rebalanced every 7 days. Flat on days
with fewer than 10 names.

COSTS
0.05% taker fee per side, slippage per side by liquidity tier (3, 8, 15 or 25 bps, src/research/portfolio
.slippage_by_liquidity), each coin's settled funding on every position.

PRIMARY OUTCOME
The annualised Sharpe ratio of the book's daily net returns over the whole window, at 1x costs, for the one
configuration above.

SECONDARY OUTCOMES (reported, not part of pass or kill unless named there)
- The return split into price legs, funding received and costs, per year.
- Sharpe per calendar year; the worst drawdown.
- A 95% confidence interval for the Sharpe from a circular block bootstrap (20-day blocks, 2,000 draws, seed 0).
- Correlation of daily returns with the taker-buy basket on the same universe, and with BTC.
- The same rule on today's Kraken list over the same window, to show what the point-in-time list changed.
- Kraken against Binance funding, 2025-10-01 to 2025-12-31 (the overlap of Kraken's history with the window): per coin
  in the universe, the correlation of daily funding, and the share of days on which the book's long and short sides
  are ordered the same way by Kraken's 7-day funding. Descriptive.

PARAMETER GRID (every configuration is a trial; count them)
9 trials, nothing else is run:
- 8 = universe size {30, 50} x quantile {0.2, 0.3} x rebalance days {7, 14}, at 1x costs. Primary: 50, 0.2, 7.
- 1 = the primary configuration at 2x costs (fee and slippage doubled).
The today's-list comparison is a configuration already logged on 2026-10-04 and is not a new trial.

PASS CRITERIA (all five)
1. Primary Sharpe >= 0.7.
2. The lower bound of its bootstrap interval is above 0.
3. Funding received over the window is positive.
4. Net return at 2x costs is positive.
5. At least 6 of the 8 grid cells have a positive Sharpe, and at least 3 of the calendar years 2022 to 2025 are
   positive for the primary configuration (2022 counts from the window's first day).

KILL IF (any)
- Primary Sharpe < 0.3.
- Net return at 2x costs is negative.
- Funding received is not positive.
Between kill and pass the result is inconclusive: the sleeve is not built.

PROSPECTIVE PHASE (only after a pass)
A pass sends the primary configuration to paper trading, not to live. The paper book charges Kraken's real funding
rates per coin. It runs 13 weeks from its first decision. That is too short to measure a Sharpe of 1 (a t-statistic
of about 0.5), so it tests the implementation, not the edge. It is stopped and the sleeve dropped if, over the 13
weeks, funding received on Kraken is not positive, or the paper book's positions differ from the backtest's
(scripts/research/paper_vs_backtest.py). Going live needs a separate decision by the owner.

KNOWN RISKS AND BIASES
- Every period of the window was seen before (PRIOR EXPOSURE).
- Perps Kraken delisted are missing from the listing snapshot: survivorship remains for those.
- Binance funding and prices are proxies for Kraken's. Kraken's funding formula and caps differ.
- The window is under four years and holds one market cycle.
- Shorting crowded small coins: squeezes, thin books, and Kraken's position limits. Capacity is small.
- Overlap with the book: the taker basket trades the same coins (correlation measured at 0.16 on today's list).
