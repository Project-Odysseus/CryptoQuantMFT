PRE-REGISTRATION H5: DERIBIT-IMPLIED PROBABILITIES PREDICT WHERE KALSHI DAILY BTC MARKETS SETTLE
Version 1. Written 2026-10-03, before any H5 test was run. Changes after results = a new version (lock it again), logged
as extra trials.

PRIOR EXPOSURE
- notebooks/prediction_markets.ipynb, run 2026-10-03: ONE live snapshot of Kalshi daily quotes next to a flat-volatility
  N(d2) value from Deribit's implied volatility. In it Kalshi quoted in-the-money levels above the model (about 0.94
  against 0.90 with a day left). No history of this comparison has been looked at.
- H3 (research log 2026-09-28): BTC implied volatility ran about 6 points above realized. Options-implied
  distributions are therefore expected to be too wide.
- The same notebook compared a realized-volatility model with Kalshi's 15-MINUTE markets (the market won). That is a
  different market and a different model.

MARKET BEHAVIOUR
Kalshi's quoted probability that BTC settles above a level differs from the probability Deribit's option prices imply
for the same level and time, and where they differ the options are partly right.

MECHANISM
Deribit's option market is deeper and professional; Kalshi's flow is largely retail, pays a fee, and cannot easily be
arbitraged against options (different venue, collateral and settlement index). Information in the option smile should
therefore reach Kalshi's quotes late or incompletely. Against this, option prices carry a volatility risk premium
(H3), which makes their implied probabilities too extreme in the tails; so the options need not be right everywhere.

OBSERVABLE PREDICTION AND EXPECTED SIGN
- gap = implied - Kalshi mid. In (outcome - Kalshi mid) = b x gap, b is above 0 (the options add information) and
  below 1 (they are not simply right).
- Mean gap is negative for levels below the price and positive for levels above it (the options' distribution is wider).

DATA
- Deribit: BTC option chain snapshots recorded about hourly by this project's collector (data/options/deribit/BTC; they
  exist from 2026-09-26, with gaps whenever the recording machine slept). Each snapshot carries Deribit's index price.
- Kalshi (public API): the daily "Bitcoin price at 5 PM New York" event, series KXBTCD, one event per day (ticker
  KXBTCD-<yy><MON><dd>17), levels every 250 USD. Quote at a snapshot = the latest 1-minute candle's Yes bid and ask
  within the 10 minutes before the snapshot; markets without a two-sided quote in that window are left out. Outcome
  and settlement value from Kalshi after settlement.
- Rows: every (snapshot, level) with the level within +-3% of Deribit's index (|ln(K/S)| <= 0.03) and 1 to 26 hours
  left to settlement. Built by src/research/hypotheses/binary_panel.py (build_panel).
- PILOT window: snapshots before 2026-10-04 00:00 UTC (already recorded when this was written). It checks that the
  pipeline works. Its numbers are reported and labelled pilot; no conclusion is drawn from them and no rule below is
  changed because of them (a change = a new version).
- CONFIRMATORY window: snapshots from 2026-10-05 00:00 UTC to 2026-11-16 00:00 UTC. None of it existed when this was
  written. It is run once, after 2026-11-17, when every market in it has settled. If it holds fewer than 20 daily
  events with rows (the recorder sleeps with the laptop), the end date moves forward day by day until it holds 20.
- This is stricter than the project's frozen-holdout rule (2026 price history is frozen for strategy research): the
  test data are in the future.

INFERENCE
Rows overlap heavily (one event at many snapshots and many levels share one outcome). Every interval is a bootstrap
over whole events (2000 resamples, 95%); rows are never treated as independent.

SIGNAL DEFINITION (at each snapshot time t; nothing after t is used)
- S = the snapshot's Deribit index price (median over the chain). T = Kalshi's settlement time.
- sigma(K) = Deribit mark implied volatility at strike K and time T: linear in strike within each listed expiry, then
  linear in total variance between the listed expiries around T (flat at the first expiry's value if T is earlier).
- implied = -(C(K + h) - C(K - h)) / (2h), C = Black-76 call at forward S with sigma(.) at that strike, h = 0.25% of K.
  This keeps the smile's slope. implied_flat = N(d2) at sigma(K) is the secondary definition.
- Kalshi mid = (Yes bid + Yes ask) / 2 as of t.

PRIMARY OUTCOME
b, the slope through the origin of (outcome - Kalshi mid) on (implied - Kalshi mid), over all settled rows of the
confirmatory window, with its event-bootstrap 95% interval.

SECONDARY OUTCOMES
- The same slope with implied_flat.
- Brier score of the Kalshi mid and of implied.
- Mean gap, Kalshi mid, implied and realized frequency in five moneyness buckets (K more than 1% below, 0.25-1% below,
  within 0.25%, 0.25-1% above, more than 1% above the index).
- Taker rule: buy Yes when implied - Yes ask - fee > 0.02; buy No when (1 - implied) - No ask - fee > 0.02; fee =
  0.07 x p x (1 - p) per contract (Kalshi's general schedule); hold to settlement. Mean profit per contract with its
  event-bootstrap interval, and the number of trades and events.

PARAMETER GRID (2 configurations)
implied (smile) and implied_flat. The 0.02 margin, the +-3% moneyness band, the 1-26 hour band and the 10-minute quote
window are fixed above. Nothing else is run.

PASS CRITERIA (confirmatory window, at least 20 events)
- b > 0 with the interval excluding 0, AND
- the taker rule's mean profit per contract > 0 with the interval excluding 0.
If only the first holds, the options carry information that the spread and the fee absorb: reported, not tradable.

KILL IF
b's interval includes 0 or lies below it.

KNOWN RISKS AND BIASES
- Different settlement numbers: Kalshi uses a 60-second average of CF Benchmarks' BRTI; S is Deribit's index.
- Quotes up to 10 minutes old; Deribit dailies expire at 08:00 UTC, so sigma is interpolated to Kalshi's time.
- About 40 events in six weeks: only a large effect can pass. Snapshots cluster in the hours the laptop is awake.
- Kalshi's fee rate is from its general schedule, not re-read for this series.
- Capacity, and whether this venue may be used at all (employer rules, country availability), are not addressed.
