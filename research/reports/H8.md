## 3. What this means

- The loss is in the price legs before any cost: a gap between two liquid coins that has opened by two standard
  deviations more often keeps widening over the next weeks than closes. At a daily horizon, relative moves between
  the large coins trend; they don't revert.
- It was least bad in flat markets, but still negative there, so it is not the flat-market strategy we were after.
- The opposite trade (follow the divergence) is suggested by this result and is therefore not evidence: it would
  need its own pre-registration and data this test has not touched. It is also close to the 30-day momentum ranking
  that was weak on this universe.
- Not tested: reversion inside the day. Daily closes and a one-day delay cannot see it; it needs intraday bars for
  the alt coins, and the earlier intraday work on BTC and ETH found nothing after fees.
