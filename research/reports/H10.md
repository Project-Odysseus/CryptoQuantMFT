## 3. Against the criteria

Failed 1 (the interval at 11 bp includes zero). Passed 2 (both halves positive), 3 (both coins positive) and 4 (all
four grid cells positive). The kill rule (gross below 5.5 bp) was not met.

## 4. What this means

- It is continuation, not reversal: the earlier study's pooled 4-hour reversal does not hold for this session. The
  sign is right barely more than half the time; the gain comes from afternoons being larger when they follow the
  morning.
- Gross is positive in all five years and all four definitions, which a pure artefact rarely is. But 15 bp a trade
  against 11 bp of fees leaves 4, with an interval from -4 to +13.
- Whether it is tradable is a question of cost: at maker fees, or a lower fee tier, the same trades keep 11 bp.
  The maker number is an upper bound, since a resting order is filled more readily when the price is moving against it.
- Leads from this data, each needing its own test on data this run has not seen: trading only after a large morning
  (+10.8 bp net), and the weekday pattern. The clean test available is the frozen 2026 window (about 190 weekdays),
  which needs the owner's approval and a version 2 of the pre-registration written before the look.
