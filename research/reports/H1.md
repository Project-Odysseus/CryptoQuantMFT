## 1. Pre-registration and deviations

`research/prereg/H1.txt`, version 1, committed (d0d038f) before any H1 result. Deviations:
- Addendum 1 (written before any result): open interest <= 0 treated as missing (Binance archive zero days).
- Deflated Sharpe: the prereg didn't fix where the cross-trial Sharpe variance comes from. Mixing the tilt (per-day

- Prior exposure (disclosed in the prereg): a 30-day funding z-score and carry had been studied on this window before.

## 3. Signal diagnostics

- z is persistent (autocorrelation 0.93 at 1 day, 0.45 at 7, ~0.1 at 30), right-skewed for BTC/ETH, left-skewed for
  SOL (the FTX collapse drove SOL funding deeply negative). z > 2 on 3.5-5.4% of days.
- Crowded days (z > 2 and OI up): BTC 51, ETH 65, SOL 44. Washed-out days (z < -1.5): 110-129 per coin.
- Spearman correlations: funding z with basis z 0.68; with dOI3 only 0.08 (OI change is separate information).

## 4. Predictive tests

Slope of the forward log return on z (bps per unit z; Newey-West per coin, Driscoll-Kraay pooled):

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

- **The sign is the opposite of the prediction** in ETH and SOL and pooled, and insignificant in BTC. The
  pre-registered kill condition (negative slope at 3 days in >= 2 of 3 coins) is met: **KILL**.
- The OI interaction is not negative as predicted (t = 0.3 at 1-3 days, -0.5 at 7 days). OI rising alone has a
  negative 1-day coefficient (-25 bps, t -2.1) — a separate, un-preregistered observation, not a result.
- Quintile sorts are U-shaped: the highest quintile has the highest 7-day mean (+273 bps) and the thinnest left
  tail (5% quantile -1,263 bps vs -1,518 to -1,952 elsewhere). Crowded funding, in this definition, marks trends,
  not fragility.
- Basis z has the same (wrong) sign as funding z.

## 8. Verdict

- **H1 directional (crowding predicts lower returns): FAIL.** Killed by the pre-registered sign test; the tilt
  fails placebo and deflated Sharpe. In this definition, high funding is a trend marker. This does not replicate the
  earlier -0.047 IC of a 30-day funding z on hourly bars, which should be treated as fragile too.
- **H1 carry at Binance fees: PASSES the development criteria** (net > 0, 2x costs > 0, 3/3 coins, 4-5 of 6
  years, placebo 100%, H1 deflated Sharpe >= 0.95), **but fails the global deflated Sharpe at 0.95** and is a regime
  trade: 2020-21 and 2024 paid, 2022 and 2025 paid nothing. At Kraken fees only the 30-day hold survives 2x costs.
- **Status: INCONCLUSIVE pending the holdout.** Expect a small or zero holdout result: 2026 funding has been low
  (research log: 1-3% a year always-on), so the timed rule has mostly been out.
- **Next step:** run the holdout once for "carry, Binance fees, 14-day hold" (the pre-registered middle config, not
  the best one picked after the fact) — needs your approval. Deployment needs spot + perp on one venue with low
  spot fees (Binance or Bybit keys; TODO.MD section 7). On Kraken the 0.40% spot fee makes it marginal.
