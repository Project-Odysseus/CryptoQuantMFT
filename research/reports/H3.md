# H3: Variance risk premium, phase A — report

Development data only (2021-03-24 to 2025-12-31). Holdout **not run**. Reproduce:
`python scripts/research/h3_study.py` (trials logged in `data/research/h3_20260928T182729Z/`; the corrected numbers
below are from the `--no-ledger` rerun `h3_20260928T183350Z/` after the funding fix described in section 1).

## 1. Pre-registration and deviations

`research/prereg/H3.txt`, version 1, committed before any H3 result. Deviations and disclosures:
- A quick summary of the VRP frame (BTC mean IV-RV ~ 6 vol points) was printed while testing the loader, after the
  prereg was committed and before the study ran. It didn't change anything, but P1 was not a blind look.
- Bug found and fixed after the first run: bar indexes (ms) and funding times (us) were compared as raw integers,
  so no funding was charged on the sleeves. The rerun charges it; it lowers every variant's Sharpe by ~0.15 and
  changes no conclusion. The fix is not a new configuration, so no trials were added.
- A2 (term-structure inversion) can't be tested: no free history of 7d/60d IV exists. Only the labelled proxy ran.

## 2. Data coverage and limitations

DVOL hourly for BTC and ETH from 2021-03-24, Binance perp 5-minute bars from 2021-03 (no gaps), 1,716 daily
observations per coin. SOL has no DVOL, so "2 of 3 assets" is "2 of 2". DVOL is a model-free 30-day index built
from the whole smile, so it sits above ATM IV; an ATM seller would earn less than DVOL - RV (matters for phase B).
Realized vol from 5-minute returns is the right comparison for a frequently hedged position.

## 7. Placebo

IV series shifted in time (300 runs): IV sizing beats 95.0% of placebos (exactly at the bar), IV debiased 87%.

## 8. Verdict

- **P1: FAIL as registered** (BTC yes, ETH no). The BTC premium is real and consistent but shrinking.
- **A1 (IV sizing): FAIL — killed** by the pre-registered criterion: it doesn't lower the matched drawdown (34.4%
  vs 32.9%), and its +0.02 Sharpe is noise. Keep EWMA sizing in the runtime.
- **A2: UNTESTED** (no term-structure history). The labelled DVOL-spike proxy hurt (Sharpe -0.12), consistent with
  the earlier finding that rising DVOL precedes higher returns. Don't add DVOL-spike risk-off filters.
- **Next step:** Phase B (short vol) is only worth scoping for BTC, and only after a bid/ask test on real chains. The
  hourly chain recorder (started 2026-09-28) builds that history; ~3 months of it lets you check whether 30-day ATM
  IV minus realized clears Deribit's spread and fees. Alternatively buy Tardis Deribit history. P2b (term
  structure) becomes testable from the same recordings after ~12 months. Phase B needs your approval.
