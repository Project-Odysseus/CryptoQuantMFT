# H15: the H13 trend design across equity, Treasury, crude oil and currency futures

Pre-registration: `research/prereg/H15.txt` (version 2). Spec: `research/strategies/multi_asset_trend.toml` (v1).
Study: `scripts/research/h15_study.py`. Run 2026-10-20. Paper book: `config/portfolio.multi_asset_trend_paper.toml`.

## Verdict: inconclusive

| criterion (all three to pass) | result | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. strategy card "evidence of an edge" | **no evidence**: Sharpe 0.39 (95% interval -0.02 to 0.81), t 1.76, timing placebo p 0.20 | fails |
| 2. Sharpe positive in both halves | 0.62 (2007-01 to 2016-06), 0.15 (2016-07 to 2025-12) | passes |
| 3. Sharpe positive at twice the costs | 0.37 | passes |

Not killed: the second half is positive. Between kill and pass, so inconclusive. The test is badly underpowered: with
19.5 years and 17 trials in the family, a true Sharpe of 0.32 (the shrunk estimate) passes 1% of the time; the
smallest Sharpe this test can detect is about 1.06. A failure here does not show the design is wrong.

## How this run came about (recorded in the ledger)

1. Version 1 used Yahoo spot rates for the currencies: Sharpe 0.35, t 1.61, halves 0.56 / 0.13, no evidence. A data check
   after the run found bad ticks (EUR/USD +17% on 2008-12-08, JPY/USD +18% on 2008-12-09).
2. Version 2 switched the currencies to Norges Bank fixings crossed to dollars. Its first run silently left out all six
   currencies: the panel cut one-price-a-day series as "close-only history". Fixed (`quantlab.data.quality`: a series
   that is one price a day throughout is used whole; `load_panel` now raises on an instrument with no bars). That run
   is noted in the ledger as not a result; its trials stay counted.
3. The run above is version 2 with all twelve markets.

All three runs' trials count against H15 (17 in the family), which is why the deflated Sharpe (0.56) is low.

## Data and its limits

- Equity: S&P 500, Nasdaq-100 and Russell 2000 price indices plus flat dividend yields, minus the US 3-month rate.
- Treasuries: IEI and IEF total return (Yahoo adjusted closes) minus the US 3-month rate. Their durations (about 4.5 and
  7.5 years) are close to, not equal to, the 5- and 10-year notes'.
- Crude: USO total return minus the US 3-month rate. USO pays the roll like the future, less a 0.7% fee, and changed how
  it rolls in 2020.
- Currencies: Norges Bank fixings (14:15 Oslo) crossed to dollars, plus the OECD 3-month rate differential.
- Contract specs for ZF, ZN and MCL are from memory and marked unconfirmed in `config/futures_markets.csv`.
