# H12: A slow version of the taker basket — report

**Verdict: INCONCLUSIVE. The slow basket traded 64% less and made 2.7 points a year less; the bar was 1 point. The
paper book keeps its rule.** One part of the hypothesis held clearly: the week-to-week changes in the taker-buy
share earn nothing, even before costs. Development data only (2022-03-23 to 2025-12-31, point-in-time Kraken list,
top 30, 20% legs). Reproduce: `python scripts/research/h12_study.py --no-ledger` (logged run
`data/research/h12_20261012T093317Z/`, 10 trials: the signal card and 9 baskets).

`research/prereg/H12.txt`, version 1 (5deb5c87eca4), locked and committed before the test. No deviations. One

## 4. What this means

- **The weekly changes in the share carry nothing.** A basket ranked only on how far a coin's 7-day share is from
  its 90-day share made -0.1% a year before costs, on turnover of 57 times a year. That is the direct test of what
  the signal card suggested, and it agrees: the basket's return is in the slow part of the score.
- **Trading the slow part once a month was not shown to be as good, nor shown to be worse.** The pre-registered run
  is 2.7 points below the baseline. Starting the same monthly schedule 10 or 20 days later gives +36.5% and +28.1%:
  the start day moves the result by 21 points, far more than the difference under test. On the longer window with
  today's coin list the slow basket is 17 points behind. The difference changes sign every year. Nothing here
  separates the two.
- **The cost saving is real and is the one firm number:** 2.7% a year against 7.2%. At twice the assumed costs the
  slow basket is ahead.
- **"A fixed set of coins" was too strong a description.** The slow basket still replaces 47% of its names at a
  monthly rebalance and held 82 different coins in under four years, because the universe itself changes (which
  coins are among the 30 most traded, and which Kraken has listed). The tilt is slow, not fixed.
- **Three coins make almost all of the net result** (section 3). The same is likely true of the baseline; it was
  not measured there. This is the small-sample problem the signal card pointed at, seen from the P&L side.
- The best cell, the slow score on the 10-day schedule (Sharpe 1.46 at half the baseline's turnover), is one of nine
  runs picked after the fact, in a test where the start day alone moves returns by 21 points. A lead, no more.

## 5. What follows

Nothing is changed in the paper book. Two leads are recorded and neither is acted on:

1. The slow score on the baseline's own 10-day schedule.
2. A monthly schedule split into three parts that rebalance 10 days apart, which removes the start-day luck that
   decided this test.

Either would be a new version of this pre-registration on data that has now been looked at, so the result would be
weak whatever it showed. The stronger evidence is the paper record the current basket is already building.
