# H2: Liquidation overshoot -> reversal (PROXY) — report

**PROXY:** no liquidation history exists; events are hours with an extreme return, an open-interest drop and a
volume spike. This tests "forced-deleveraging-shaped hours revert", not liquidations directly.
Development data only (2021-11 to 2025-12-31). Holdout **not run**. Reproduce:
`python scripts/research/h2_study.py` (trials logged in `data/research/h2_20260928T183212Z/`; the corrected numbers
below are from the `--no-ledger` rerun `h2_20260928T183350Z/`).

## 1. Pre-registration and deviations

`research/prereg/H2.txt`, version 1, committed before any H2 result. Deviations:
- Same timestamp-unit bug as H3 in the first run (no funding charged, news split wrong: every event flagged). Fixed
  and rerun without adding trials; event-study numbers are unchanged, trade net returns moved by about +4 bps.
- News split uses FOMC statements only. CPI release dates couldn't be fetched (bls.gov refuses scripts, FRED timed
  out) and were not typed in from memory. See `research/data/README.md`.

## 3. Signal diagnostics

127 long-liquidation-shaped events and 73 squeeze-shaped. Exhaustion (volume back under 2x median) came within 6
hours for 80% of events (median wait 4 hours); the rest entered at t+7. 10 of 200 events fall within [-2h, +4h] of an FOMC statement.

## 7. Placebo

1,000 sets of random vol-matched pseudo-events: p = 0.17 / 0.30 / 0.74. Needs < 0.05.

## 8. Verdict

- **H2 (proxy): FAIL.** CAR isn't distinguishable from the baseline, and the trades lose at stressed costs (before
  even doubling them). Both pre-registered kill conditions hold.
- Opposite to the prediction, the 10 FOMC-window events reverted strongly (+170-200 bps CAR). With 10 events this is
  an anecdote, not a finding; it would need its own pre-registration and far more events.
- **This does not reject the liquidation mechanism**, only this proxy of it. The real test needs recorded
  liquidations: the recorder has been running since 2026-09-28 (Binance + Bybit, BTC/ETH/SOL). After 6-12 months,
  first check whether proxy events coincide with liquidation notional > 5x median, then re-run H2 v2 on real
  liquidation events. Alternatively, buy liquidation history (Tardis, Coinalyze) if you want an answer sooner.
