# H6: Cross-sectional funding carry on Kraken-tradable perps — report

**Verdict: INCONCLUSIVE. By the pre-registration the sleeve is not built and does not go to paper trading.**
Development data only (2022-03-23 to 2025-12-31). Holdout not run. Reproduce: `python scripts/research/h6_study.py
--no-ledger` (the logged run is `data/research/h6_20261004T075300Z/`, 9 trials).

## 1. Pre-registration and deviations

`research/prereg/H6.txt`, version 1 (616f20abf16a), locked and committed before the test was run. No deviations.
The Kraken funding comparison uses the book's daily target sides, not the positions held between weekly rebalances.

## 2. Data

1,380 days. 35 coins on a median day (10 to 48): the 50 most traded Binance perps, kept only from the day after
Kraken opened its own perp. Kraken's linear perps began 2022-03-22, so 2020 and 2021, the best years in the earlier
studies, are not in the window. Perps Kraken has delisted are missing.

## 4. Kraken against Binance funding (2025-10-01 to 2025-12-31, 66 coins)

Median daily correlation per coin 0.23. Kraken ordered the book's two sides the same way on 84% of 88 days, but the
funding spread between them was +30.6% a year on Kraken against +89.4% on Binance. On Kraken the book would have
collected about a third of the funding the backtest credits it with. Funding is nearly all of the net return, so
this alone would take most of it away.

## 6. What this means

- The earlier numbers (1.09 and 0.93 in the signal lab) came mostly from 2020-2021, before Kraken had these perps,
  and partly from using today's coin list. On what could have been traded, the Sharpe is 0.5 with an interval that
  includes zero.
- The funding leg is real but small after costs, and smaller again on Kraken. The book's result is a bet on the
  price legs of small coins.
- Not built. Worth reopening only with Kraken's own funding history over a longer span (it is being published
  about a year back; store it from now on), or on a venue where the funding spread is what Binance shows.
